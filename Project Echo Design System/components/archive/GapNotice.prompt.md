Use for every empty or partial state: roster-only stations, no schedules imported, no hearings recorded, no receiver saved.

```jsx
<GapNotice title="Roster entry" links={[{ label: 'Priyom.org', url: '…', description: 'Logs and schedules maintained by the community.' }]}>
  The designator, name, operator and status are sourced; frequencies, schedules and history have not been imported yet.
</GapNotice>
```

Say what is missing and why, then link onward. Never render an empty frequency table as though the station had no frequencies, and never use an illustration or a shrug — the gap is in the import, not in the station.
